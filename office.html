Executive desks

The Office Collection

Photographed in our showroom. Prices on request.

Graphite Executive Desk

Wide graphite desk with a dark top and a lit, rounded front panel. A matching tall bookcase with open lit shelves stands behind it.

Walnut Light-Line Desk

Walnut-toned desk with a warm light strip running through the front panel. Pairs with an open-shelf bookcase and a wood feature wall.

Long rounded desk top with a curved side return cabinet. The bookcase has lit shelves and two tall rosewood end panels.
